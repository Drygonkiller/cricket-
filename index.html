How to play

  1. In Chase, the AI sets your target. In Defend, you bat first and then bowl.
  2. Aim Cover, Straight or Leg with a button, an arrow key or a tap on the pitch.
  3. Press HIT or Space as the ball reaches the gold line.
  4. Match the line of the ball with perfect timing to hit a six.
  5. To bowl, choose your line and press BOWL or Space in the green zone.
